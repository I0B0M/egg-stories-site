Egg Stories publisher

Egg Stories is a personal video-production and YouTube publishing tool operated for its owner's channels. It prepares original narrated animated stories, checks their source and media evidence, and uploads approved outputs to the exact channel the owner connected. It can verify processing, apply a cover, schedule releases and retrieve channel/video analytics for later review.

Google sign-in authorizes access to the selected channel. It does not transfer ownership of the channel. Egg Shorts use automated release checks; other channels retain their own approval policy. The app is not offered as a public login service.
